# Project Governance

The NC Smart-City Systems Atlas is designed as a source-linked community research project.

## Decision principles

1. Public evidence stays attached to every published record.
2. Conservative classification is preferred to inflated adoption claims.
3. Municipal confirmation is distinguished from vendor and secondary reporting.
4. Corrections are welcome and preserved in a dated changelog.
5. Surveillance and public-safety technologies require privacy, civil-rights, retention, oversight, and outcome context.
6. The atlas documents evidence; it does not endorse a product, vendor, municipality, or policy.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers review URL safety, source relevance, duplicate candidates, lifecycle evidence, AI classifications, and privacy risks before release. Conflicts should be documented, and ambiguous records should remain in review rather than being forced into a definitive category.

## Release process

Each release should include a version, snapshot date, generated date, sanitized data exports, validation results, and a human-readable changelog. Publication remains subject to ownership and release-rights review for the original internship material.
