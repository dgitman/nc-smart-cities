# Project Governance

Public evidence stays attached to every record. Conservative classification is preferred to inflated adoption claims. Municipal confirmation is distinguished from vendor and secondary reporting. Corrections enter dated releases and changelogs.

Surveillance and public-safety technologies should include privacy, civil-rights, retention, oversight, and outcome context. The atlas documents evidence; it does not endorse products, vendors, municipalities, or policies.

Publication remains subject to ownership and release-rights review for the original internship material.
