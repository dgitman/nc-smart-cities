# Contributing to the NC Smart-City Systems Atlas

Propose one missing system, correction, stronger source, lifecycle update, or AI review at a time. Every proposal needs a direct public HTTP(S) source that supports the jurisdiction-specific claim.

Do not submit internal Town links, SharePoint or personal-drive links, email deep links, credentials, private pricing, operational security details, staff contact fields, or raw notes from a private work environment.

Review proceeds from proposal to source check, classification review, and a dated published release. Do not infer active AI from vendor capability, analytics, automation, cameras, or “smart” branding alone.
