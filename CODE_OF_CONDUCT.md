# Community Code of Conduct

We welcome evidence-based additions, corrections, questions, and disagreement.

Participants should be respectful, specific, and willing to distinguish public source facts from inference or opinion. Do not use the project to target municipal staff, disclose personal information, expose security-sensitive details, or promote a vendor without transparent evidence.

Maintainers may remove contributions that contain private information, unsafe links, unsupported claims, harassment, discrimination, or conflicts of interest that are not disclosed.
