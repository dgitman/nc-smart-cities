# Contributing to the NC Smart-City Systems Atlas

Thank you for helping improve this source-linked community dataset.

## What you can contribute

- A missing municipal system or initiative
- A correction to an existing record
- A stronger or more current public source
- A lifecycle update
- A review of an AI role or evidence label
- Privacy, civil-rights, governance, accessibility, or outcome evidence

## Evidence requirements

Every proposed record must include at least one direct public HTTP(S) source. The link must open without a private account, employee login, invitation, or personal-drive permission. A source should support the jurisdiction-specific claim—not merely describe a vendor’s general capabilities.

Do not submit internal Town links, SharePoint or personal-drive links, email deep links, credentials, private pricing, operational security details, staff contact fields, or raw notes from a private work environment.

## Review process

1. Submit one system or correction per structured proposal.
2. A reviewer checks URL safety and the jurisdiction-specific claim.
3. A reviewer confirms pillar, lifecycle, audience, and AI classification.
4. Accepted changes enter a dated release and changelog.

## AI classification

Do not infer active AI from vendor capability, analytics, automation, cameras, “smart” branding, predictive marketing language, or broad platform documentation. Distinguish explicit AI-primary use, AI-assisted workflow, AI-capable but unconfirmed use, and unresolved relevance.

## Attribution

Contributors may be credited in release notes unless they request anonymity. Source authorship and municipal ownership should never be implied by contribution credit.
