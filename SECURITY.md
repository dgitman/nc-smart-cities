# Security and Sensitive Information Policy

## Do not submit

- Credentials, secrets, access tokens, or password-like values
- Internal Town, SharePoint, OneDrive, Drive, Gmail, Teams, or login-gated links
- Private or link-local network addresses
- Staff contact lists, credential identifiers, or access-control records
- Nonpublic system configuration, infrastructure weaknesses, camera or access layouts, security operations, or remediation steps
- Private pricing, licensing, or procurement detail

## Public URL policy

Published records must use public HTTP(S) links. The build rejects internal collaboration hosts, local and private addresses, personal or administrative paths, URL user information, and secret-like query parameters. Rejected URL values must never be logged or copied into public artifacts.

## Reporting a concern

Until a public repository and private reporting channel are approved, do not send sensitive details through a public issue or contribution. Preserve the material securely and contact the project maintainer through an approved private channel.

## Scope

This policy governs the community atlas and its public data exports. It does not authorize publication of the original internship source files.
